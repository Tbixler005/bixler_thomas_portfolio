Research methods lab · course

Intellectual Humility & Apology Quality

  • 2×2 between-subjects
  • N = 108
  • Qualtrics + Prolific
  • Pearson r · two-way ANOVA
  • Co-authored APA paper

A 2×2 between-subjects experiment I designed, ran, and analyzed for my research methods course, testing whether intellectual humility and a victim's response affect apology quality. Built and administered the study in Qualtrics; recruited via Prolific and convenience sampling; analyzed with a Pearson correlation and a two-way ANOVA. Found a significant positive correlation between intellectual humility and apology quality, with null experimental effects traced to a weak manipulation, reported openly. Co-authored the full APA paper.
