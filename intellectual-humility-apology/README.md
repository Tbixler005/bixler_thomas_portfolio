# Intellectual Humility & Post-Transgression Response on Apology Quality

**Research Methods Lab · Spring 2026 · University of Pittsburgh**
Co-authored with Jacob Bayer

A 2×2 between-subjects experiment (N = 108) replicating and extending Ludwig et
al. (2022). We manipulated **intellectual humility** (a reflection task vs. a
control writing task) and the **victim's post-transgression response** (forgiving
vs. unforgiving) to test their effects on the quality of a transgressor's apology.
Measured intellectual humility showed a significant positive correlation with
apology quality, *r*(104) = .37, *p* < .001. The experimental manipulations,
however, produced null main effects and no interaction, a result we reported and
interpreted openly, tracing it to a likely weak manipulation rather than
downplaying it.

Below is the full path to the final paper, in the order the work was completed.
Each step fed into the finished manuscript.
